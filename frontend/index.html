            Legal Compliance Manual
            

              Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011
            

            

              Under the Legal Metrology Act, 2009, every pre-packaged commodity manufactured, packed, imported, or offered for sale in India must bear mandatory statutory declarations. This platform automates compliance verification using state-of-the-art vision models to prevent consumer deception and ensure strict statutory adherence.
            

          

          

            

              
              
PCR 2011 & Act 2009

              Statutory Enforcement Framework
            

          

        

      


      
      

        

          
1

          
The 5 Mandatory Declarations Under PCR, 2011

        


        

          
          

            

              

                 Rule 6(1)(e)
                Price Declaration
              

              

                
MRP & Tax Declaration

                

                  The Maximum Retail Price (MRP) must be clearly printed in digits and accompanied by the mandatory statutory phrase:
                

                

                  "Maximum Retail Price ₹ / Rs. ... Incl. of all taxes"
                

                
    
                  
  • Prohibits charging above declared MRP under any pretext.
    • 
                  
  • Disallows dual MRP sticker overwriting without statutory authorization.
    • 
                  
  • Must clearly state "inclusive of all taxes" or "incl. of GST".
    • 
                

              

            

          


          
          

            

              

                 Rule 6(1)(c) & Rule 12
                Net Quantity
              

              

                
Net Quantity & Standard Units

                

                  The net quantity contained in the package must be expressed in standard units of weight, measure, or numerical count:
                

                

                  "Net Qty: 500 g" / "1 L" / "10 N" / "368 Pages"
                

                
    
                  
  • Units must strictly adhere to the metric system (g, kg, ml, l, m).
    • 
                  
  • Count-based commodities must use the statutory symbol 'N' or 'U'.
    • 
                  
  • Subject to maximum permissible error (MPE) thresholds specified in the Second Schedule.
    • 
                

              

            

          


          
          

            

              

                 Rule 6(1)(d)
                Date of Packaging
              

              

                
Date of Mfg / Packing / Import

                

                  Every package must specify the month and year in which the commodity is manufactured, pre-packed, or imported:
                

                

                  "Mfg Date: 05/2026" / "Pkd: Jan 2026"
                

                
    
                  
  • Mandatory format: MM/YYYY or Month YYYY.
    • 
                  
  • Crucial for monitoring product shelf-life and preventing sale of expired commodities.
    • 
                  
  • For imported commodities, the month and year of import must be explicitly stated.
    • 
                

              

            

          


          
          

            

              

                 Rule 6(1)(a) & (b)
                Origin & Liability
              

              

                
Manufacturer / Packer / Importer Details

                

                  The complete name and physical address of the entity responsible for manufacturing or packaging must be declared:
                

                

                  "Manufactured & Packed by: ABC Foods Pvt. Ltd., Plot 42, Sector 5, Industrial Area, Gurugram, HR - 122001"
                

                
    
                  
  • Must contain complete physical location (street, city, state, and postal PIN code).
    • 
                  
  • If manufactured by one party and marketed by another, both entities must be declared.
    • 
                  
  • For imported products, the complete name and address of the importer is mandatory.
    • 
                

              

            

          


          
          

            

              

                 Rule 6(1)(f)
                Consumer Redressal
              

              

                
Consumer Care Contact Details

                

                  Clear consumer redressal mechanism must be printed on the principal display panel or side panel:
                

                

                  "For Consumer Complaints: Manager - Customer Care, Tel: 1800-XXX-XXXX, Email: care@domain.com, Address: [Same as Mfg]"
                

                
    
                  
  • Must declare the designation or name of the grievance officer.
    • 
                  
  • Must provide both a valid working telephone/toll-free number and an electronic mail address.
    • 
                  
  • Ensures consumer dispute resolution is accessible and transparent.
    •

Statutory Violations, Penalties & Enforcement Powers

        


        

          
          

            

              

                 Section 36(1)
                Financial Penalties
              

              

                
Non-Standard Packaging Penalties

                

                  Whoever manufactures, packs, distributes, sells, or delivers any non-standard or non-compliant package under PCR 2011 is punishable with:
                

                

                  First Offence:
                  Fine up to ₹25,000
                

                

                  Second Offence:
                  Fine up to ₹50,000
                

                

                  Subsequent Offences:
                  Fine up to ₹1,00,000 or Imprisonment up to 1 Year (or both)
                

              

            

          


          
          

            

              

                 Section 48
                Compounding
              

              

                
Compounding of Offences

                

                  Any offence punishable under the Legal Metrology Act, 2009 may be compounded before or after the institution of prosecution by an authorized Legal Metrology Officer.
                

                
    
                  
  • Compounding fee is determined based on the severity and nature of the declaration omission.
    • 
                  
  • Once compounded, no further court proceedings shall be taken against the offender for that specific batch.
    • 
                  
  • Compounding is generally not granted for repeated willful violations.
    • 
                

              

            

          


          
          

            

              

                 Section 15 & Rule 32
                Seizure Powers
              

              

                
Inspection, Seizure & Notice

                

                  Authorized Legal Metrology Officers and Enforcement Inspectors possess statutory powers under Section 15 of the Act and Rule 32 of PCR, 2011:
                

                
    
                  
  • Premises Inspection: Right to enter retail stores, wholesale hubs, warehouses, and factories.
    • 
                  
  • Consignment Seizure: Power to seize non-compliant packages, labels, and packaging materials.
    • 
                  
  • Show Cause Notice: Issue a statutory notice allowing 7 to 15 days for explanation or rectification.
    •
